How to use this dashboard

  • Use the step tabs (1–5) at the top to move through hazard, capacity, poverty, overlap, and priority views.
  • Hover a province on the map to see a floating tooltip with key indicators.
  • Double-click a province on the map to pin that tooltip so it stays visible when you move the pointer away. Press Esc to dismiss, or hover a different province to replace the pin.
  • Single-click a province to open the detail card (use × to close).
  • Drag the map to pan and use +/− or the scroll wheel to zoom (double-click is reserved for pinning the tooltip).
  • Use Compare A / B and storm filters in the left panel to focus the map, charts, and comparison table.
